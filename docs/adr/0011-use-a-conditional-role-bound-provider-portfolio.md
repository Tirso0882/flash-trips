# Use a conditional, role-bound provider portfolio

Flash Trips will activate an external provider only for bounded roles and tested markets after access, commercial and retention terms, attribution, deep-link behaviour, offline conformance, live probes, and worst-case cost accounting have passed. A versioned Provider Market Profile records the role, origin and destination region, date horizon, mode, currency, observed coverage, link success, latency, probe date, and activation decision. The product will not claim global provider support or advertise a capability in a market with no activated production provider.

The initial conditional portfolio is:

- **Air Travel:** Skyscanner Flights is the primary because its redirect links fit the non-booking boundary. It has no production fallback until an equivalent redirect provider qualifies. Duffel remains development evaluation only while its hosted links expire after 24 hours and its normal flow expects orders.
- **Accommodation:** Booking.com Demand is primary, with Skyscanner Hotels as the only initial equivalent fallback. Hotelbeds and Expedia Rapid remain outside production because their documented flows own booking operations; Expedia Redirect is unavailable to new applicants.
- **Dining and Activities place discovery:** Google Places is primary, with Foursquare as a conditional fallback. Neither activates until reviewed terms permit the minimum immutable Evidence record. Public Nominatim and Overpass are not equivalent rich-place inventory.
- **Route Measurement:** Google Routes is primary for transit and traffic-aware routes. Hosted openrouteservice may fall back for driving, walking, cycling, hiking, and wheelchair modes only; there is no initial transit fallback.
- **Weather:** commercial Open-Meteo is primary, with OpenWeather as an explicitly licensed fallback.
- **Foreign exchange:** ExchangeRate-API is primary. ECB reference rates may fall back only for supported currencies and applicable business dates.
- **General search:** Tavily is primary, with Brave Search as a conditional fallback. Search results remain Provider Observations; the cited source must be fetched and validated separately before it may become Evidence.

Safety and health sources use an explicit, demand-driven Advisory Authority registry rather than a fixed nationality list. Trip Intake lets the Planner select one or more applicable authorities for each Planner or Companion; Flash Trips never infers an authority from External Identity, email, language, location, nationality, or model output. Runtime search may propose official URLs, but normalized authority-specific Evidence requires an Operator-reviewed registry entry defining the audience, allowed domains, coverage, extraction, freshness, correction, attribution, retention, operational, and safety contracts. Different authorities remain separate and keep their native terminology. Flash Trips does not average warning levels; any authority advising against the planned leisure Trip triggers the applicable safety Approval gate.

Health Evidence has separate global, regional, and authority-specific layers. WHO Disease Outbreak News is the conditional global baseline after commercial-use and immutable-retention permission; its non-exhaustive event set cannot prove the absence of risk. ECDC is the preferred European supplement after adapter validation, while PAHO is an Americas supplement only after commercial permission. Authority adapters are implemented in the order required by invited Planners; Poland GIS and Colombia MinSalud/INS are conditional first needs, while CDC, TravelHealthPro, Canada, Australia, and later authorities follow the same registry process. At least one current source applicable to the destination is required, together with every authority-specific source the Planner marked mandatory. Missing optional authority coverage is a visible typed limitation; missing required coverage returns `Blocked`.

An adapter owns authentication, provider requests, pagination, native schemas, and transport failures, and emits a source-specific Provider Observation. The owning capability validates identity, scope, freshness, legal eligibility, and Evidence sufficiency before creating Candidates or Evidence. Canonical source identity is `(provider, provider identifier)`. Deterministic entity resolution may deduplicate physical places only from strong identity and location Evidence while preserving every provenance record; flight and accommodation offers, prices, terms, and links are never merged by apparent similarity.

Only one provider is queried initially for a capability. At most one declared fallback expansion may occur when the primary is unavailable, exhausts allowlisted retries, lacks activated market coverage, or returns fewer eligible Candidates than the capability contract requires. A fallback must satisfy the same schema, Evidence, attribution, and deep-link policy. Search, model memory, booking APIs, or weaker provider classes cannot substitute for inventory, official advisories, Route Measurements, weather, or foreign exchange. Fallback occurs only during Candidate discovery; provider failure after a Candidate becomes a Selection derives `Revalidation Required` and starts a new Run rather than silently replacing it.

Initial Evidence-policy maxima are fifteen minutes for flight and accommodation offers, with provider revalidation before the Approval Request; thirty days for place identity and coordinates; twenty-four hours for business status and opening constraints; five minutes for traffic-aware routes; twenty-four hours for static road routes; six hours for transit schedules; six hours for official safety advisories, structured outbreak feeds, and weather observations; twenty-four hours for authority health pages and foreign exchange; and the source issue date for weekly professional health reports used only as historical context. Weather must remain inside the provider forecast horizon. Provider expiry and cache metadata may only shorten these limits. Applicable safety and health sources are rechecked before Approval and handbook compilation.

Every activated adapter is bound to a versioned terms and attribution manifest recording reviewed terms, review date, permitted retention, required attribution, link and tracking rules, market constraints, fixture policy, and next review. Required affiliate parameters may appear only from trusted configuration, must be disclosed, contain no Planner or Trip identifier, and cannot influence Candidate eligibility or ranking. Provider ordering, recommendations, and sponsorship are untrusted metadata; deterministic Flash Trips policy controls ranking, budget, and feasibility.

Inventory, place, and route Provider Market Profiles expire after thirty days and immediately after material terms, schema, adapter, attribution, or deep-link changes. Other providers use a risk-based recorded next-review date. Deactivation blocks new work, preserves historical records, and derives `Revalidation Required` for affected Evidence, Selections, Approvals, and Handbook Snapshots. Unsupported markets, absent eligible inventory, and deliberately inactive providers return `Blocked`; malformed responses, invalid credentials, contract violations, schema drift, and exhausted technical dependencies return `Failed`. Bounded allowlisted retries and the declared fallback precede terminal technical failure.

Adapters receive only the locations, dates, party counts, modes, currencies, and bounded preferences required for a call. They never receive names, External Identity, contact details, full conversation history, unrelated Trip facts, or Advisory Authority ownership. Each attempt records provider request identifiers, billable units, bounded cost, quota metadata, latency, retries, and fallback use without sensitive payloads. Provider-specific resource ceilings remain owned by the model-routing and external-cost decision.

The initial portfolio defers direct booking and order APIs, reservation management, Expedia and Amadeus enterprise integrations, Hotelbeds production certification, self-hosted OSM or openrouteservice infrastructure, provider photos and review text, Mapbox, WeatherAPI, Azure OpenAI web search, automated interpretation of arbitrary government pages, and simultaneous multi-provider price aggregation.
