# Adapt Wanderlisted evaluations without inheriting baselines

Flash Trips will reuse Wanderlisted's four-layer evaluation model and suitable cases as seed material, but it will adapt them to Flash Trips contracts and will not treat Wanderlisted runners, caches, trajectories, or baselines as evidence of Flash Trips quality. All live capture and model-judge execution must also fail closed without explicit approval; this sacrifices drop-in reuse so evaluation results remain attributable to the system actually being tested.
